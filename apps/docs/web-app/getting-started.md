# Getting Started

Start with one location and a few clothes you wear often. Your [Account](./account-and-support) page shows your current allowance; checkout and credit purchases are unavailable.

## Sign in and choose your workspace

Open [Sky Style](https://skystyle.app), select **Get started free**, and sign in with GitHub or Google. The first-use tour lets you choose Style, Shop, or both. It highlights the real controls without generating advice or consuming allowance. You can skip it and replay it from [Settings → Tutorials](./settings#tutorials).

The no-login **Try Live Demo** on the landing page shows weather. A signed-in demo account is a separate preview feature; its limits and temporary storage differ from a real account.

## Choose a location

In Style, use **Use my location**, or search for a city or enter coordinates. Manual location works without GPS permission. Sharing location text with AI is optional: weather still needs coordinates when that option is off. See [location preferences](./settings#preferences).

## Get your first outfit

Use the basic controls first. Choose an occasion if useful, then select **Fetch Weather & Style**. The result shows an outfit, its reasoning and the weather used. Model, provider and custom-source settings are advanced options.

The 6.3 development preview also shows an illustrative outfit before the first request. That example uses no AI and no allowance. See [Everyday Outfits](./daily-outfits) for the preview's availability and daily-use controls.

## Refine the result

Ask a follow-up such as “Should I bring an umbrella?” or “Can I wear formal shoes?” Replace mode updates the current advice; Chat mode keeps a conversation below it. Choose the mode in [Settings](./settings#dashboard-behaviour).

Clothing links identify items in your closet or prepare an editable Shop query. Opening a link does not start an AI request or search. See [Shop](./shop).

## Add your clothes

Open **Closet** and add descriptions such as “White Oxford shirt”, “Navy chinos” and “Black Chelsea boots”. In the development preview, add category, colour, warmth and waterproofing when useful, and mark items unavailable when they are in the wash.

Adding or editing clothes does not consume the “owned clothes only” recommendation counter. That counter applies when requesting restricted outfit advice. See the [usage reference](./how-it-works#usage-limits).

## Keep useful outfits

In the development preview, save a favourite, reopen it from **Saved outfits**, or replace one item. A manual swap uses no AI; an AI swap uses a follow-up allowance after a usable replacement is returned. Saved advice reflects the conditions when it was created. See [Everyday Outfits](./daily-outfits).
