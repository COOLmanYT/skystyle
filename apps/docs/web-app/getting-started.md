# Getting Started

New to Sky Style? This walkthrough takes you from sign-up to your first refined recommendation in a few minutes.

## Step 1 — Create an account

1. Go to [skystyle.app](https://skystyle.app).
2. Click **Get started free** (or **Try Live Demo** to preview weather data without an account).
3. Sign in with **GitHub** or **Google**.

You start on the **Free** plan: 20 AI recommendations, 40 follow-ups, and 4 closet uses per day. See [Plans](./account-and-support#plans).

## Step 2 — Take the dashboard tour

On your first visit to the dashboard, a guided tour appears automatically. It has three steps:

1. **Choose a location** — use the weather panel to enter a location or use GPS.
2. **Set your preferences** — adjust planning and closet options before generating an outfit.
3. **Continue the conversation** — use follow-ups to refine an outfit once it is ready.

You can **replay the tour** anytime from [Settings → Tutorials](./settings#tutorials).

## Step 3 — Pick a location

On the dashboard's weather panel:

- Click **Use my location** to grant GPS access for the most accurate local weather, **or**
- Type a city or place name into the search box and pick from the matches (geocoded via OpenStreetMap).

Your location consent is controlled in [Settings → Preferences](./settings#preferences). If you don't consent to sharing your location with the AI, Sky Style still fetches weather for your coordinates but won't include your location text in the AI prompt.

## Step 4 — Generate your first recommendation

1. (Optional) Choose a **recommendation mode** in the Weather Planning panel: Simple, Simple+, Advanced, or Pro. Simple is fine to start.
2. Click **Generate** (or the equivalent action on the panel).
3. Sky Style fetches weather, builds a prompt, and calls the AI. You'll see the outfit, the reasoning, and the weather used.

::: tip What you'll see
The result includes the **outfit** (concise text), **reasoning** (why those choices suit the weather), the **weather snapshot** used, and the **model** that generated it.
:::

## Step 5 — Ask a follow-up

Refine the recommendation by asking a follow-up question — for example:

- "Should I bring an umbrella?"
- "What if I need formal shoes?"
- "It's for an outdoor wedding — how should I adjust?"

Follow-ups run in one of two modes ([set in Settings](./settings#dashboard-behaviour)):

- **Replace mode** — the follow-up overwrites the current outfit.
- **Chat mode** — follow-ups stack below, keeping a conversation.

You have **40 follow-ups per day** on the Free plan.

## Step 6 — Add clothes to your closet

So recommendations match what you actually own:

1. Open the **Closet** page (`/closet`) from the navigation.
2. Add items (e.g. "White Oxford shirt", "Navy chinos", "Black Chelsea boots").
3. Future recommendations can reference your closet items.

Closet usage is limited to **4 uses per day** on the Free plan (the closet panel can be opened from the dashboard too).

## Step 7 — Explore your account

- **[Settings](./settings)** — set your gender context, units, theme, and dashboard behaviour.
- **[Security & Privacy](./security-and-privacy)** — enable 2FA, add a passkey, or manage your data.
- **[Account](./account-and-support)** — view your plan, usage, and credits.
- **[Automatic Recommendations](./automatic-recommendations)** — schedule a recommendation for a specific time.

## Daily limits at a glance

| Counter | Free | Resets |
| --- | --- | --- |
| AI recommendations | 20/day | Midnight UTC |
| Follow-ups | 40/day | Midnight UTC |
| Closet uses | 4/day | Midnight UTC |
| Source picks | 4/day | Midnight UTC |
| Model switches | 2/day | Midnight UTC |

When you approach a limit, the dashboard shows your usage. See [How It Works → Usage limits](./how-it-works#usage-limits) for the full table across plans.
